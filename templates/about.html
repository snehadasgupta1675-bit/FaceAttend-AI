ABOUT FACEATTEND AI

A focused attendance system where every page has one purpose.

FaceAttend AI is a Django-based attendance application designed around a simple separation: student information belongs in Students, while all live face-camera work belongs in Attendance.

◉One camera locationFace capture is intentionally kept on the Attendance page.
THE IDEA

Separate information from recognition.

A student record starts with basic information such as Student ID, name, email and course. The registration form stays clean because it does not ask for a face image.

When the record is ready, Attendance becomes the camera workspace. A live frame can be saved as the student's face profile, and later fresh frames can be used for daily recognition.
